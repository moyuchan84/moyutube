My Kid Asked: Doctor, Pharmacist, Nurse — What's the Difference?

Waiting at the pharmacy counter, my eight-year-old asked how doctors, pharmacists and nurses are different. I said the doctor is the boss and the pharmacist "just sells medicine" — right in front of the pharmacist. So I looked it up: a 13th-century emperor's law that split doctors from pharmacies, Korea's separation of prescribing and dispensing in 2000, and Florence Nightingale's lamp in 1854. Not a ladder, but different places around one sick person. Korean audio with English subtitles.
