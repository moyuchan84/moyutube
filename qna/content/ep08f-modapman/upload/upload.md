"Dad, if a thief gets caught, do they go straight to jail?"
Dad answered with total confidence — and racked up four wrong answers at the police box.
With Korea's Prosecutors' Office shut down after 78 years, what do the police, prosecutors and judges each do now? Explained in 3 minutes.

✔ Police send people to jail? → Police find and investigate. A judge decides on jail.
✔ Arrest warrant denied = innocent? → It only means there's no need to lock them up. The case goes on.
✔ Won't pay back a loan = arrest? → Lied from the start = fraud (criminal). Just can't pay = civil.
✔ After the reform → Police investigate, the Serious Crimes Investigation Agency takes a few major crimes, prosecutors (now at the Public Prosecution Office) request warrants and indict but can't investigate.
✔ In the end, the judge always decides.
